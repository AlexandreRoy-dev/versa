About

A strategic partner, not only a financing file.

Versa Capital is an advisory firm focused on commercial financing and equipment financing. We look at each situation as a whole in order to propose solutions that are suited to the business, durable and competitive.

We take the time to understand the business, its model and its objectives. Our aim is to deliver the quality of our service, without compromise, and to build relationships that are solid, lasting and productive.

Our history

Founded in 2022, Versa Capital has grown since it began. The firm moved from a service centred mainly on equipment financing to a strategic partner for its clients, its brokers and its business partners.
