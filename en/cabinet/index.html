How it works

What an application looks like

Four steps, one point of contact. You only file the paperwork once.

  1. 1

    You describe the project

    By phone, by email or through the form. The equipment, an approximate amount and your timeline are enough to get started.

  2. 2

    A broker calls you back within 24 hours

    They confirm your needs, your financial situation and which solution actually holds up before approaching any institution.

  3. 3

    We shop and negotiate

    Your file goes to our financial partners. We negotiate rates and terms, then come back to you with offers you can compare side by side.

  4. 4

    You sign, the equipment runs

    We coordinate the paperwork between the selected institution and your supplier, through to disbursement.
