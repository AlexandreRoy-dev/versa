Agricultural equipment financing

For businesses in Quebec that finance agricultural equipment, the asset of the Agriculture sector.

The need

Farm equipment, not job-site machinery

The Agriculture sector on the site is about agricultural equipment. This page stays with that asset. Construction, excavation or forestry machinery is treated separately, on the heavy machinery financing page.

How long the asset will be held guides the structure. Equipment the business expects to keep for a long time and equipment planned for a shorter term do not necessarily lead to the same contract. That is already a criterion of equipment financing.

What is at stake

  • Agricultural equipment in the Agriculture sector
  • How long the asset will be held
  • Access to more than 20 financial partners

According to the planned term

Keep the asset, or use it for a term

Toward ownership

If the business aims to own the equipment, the file can be a term loan to purchase or a capital lease. The end-of-term terms are those of the contract.

For a term, with no buyout

If the use is planned for the length of the contract, with no commitment to buy, an operating lease with no purchase guarantee is the form already in the catalogue. Nothing requires the business to buy the asset at the end.

Outside any promise

Calendar and rates

Versa Capital does not publish a seasonal calendar, a down payment or a rate for agricultural equipment. Those elements, if they exist, come from a partner's written offer, not from a rule posted here.

Agricultural equipment that is already paid for can be reviewed in a refinancing file. The accounting and tax treatment depends on the contract. An accountant or a tax advisor must confirm it.

To remember

  • Agriculture sector: agricultural equipment
  • The planned term guides the contract
  • No rate and no calendar are posted here

FAQ

Common questions

Which equipment does this page cover?

Agricultural equipment in the Agriculture sector. Job-site machinery and forestry equipment are on another page.

Does the choice of contract depend on how long the equipment will be used?

Yes. How long the asset will be held is already one of the criteria Versa Capital uses to structure equipment financing.

Can leasing be used for agricultural equipment?

A capital lease and an operating lease with no purchase guarantee are part of the catalogue. The contract states the form that is kept.

Does Versa Capital show payments adapted to the seasons?

No. No payment calendar is published on this page. Only a partner offer, once the file has been reviewed, can contain one.

Who compares the partners?

Versa Capital presents the file to more than 20 financial partners and explains the options to the business, with one point of contact.

Is an accountant needed?

Yes, before signing, for the accounting and tax treatment of the contract. Versa Capital does not give tax advice.
