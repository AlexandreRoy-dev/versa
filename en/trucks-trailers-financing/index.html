The need

Transportation equipment in the catalogue

The Transportation sector on the site is about transportation equipment. Here, that means trucks and trailers, two assets used for the movement of the business.

A truck and a trailer can be presented in the same file or in two files. Versa Capital does not decide that split in advance: it follows the need described and what the partners agree to review.

What is at stake

  • Trucks, as transportation equipment
  • Trailers, in the same file or apart
  • Access to more than 20 financial partners

The two assets

The truck and the trailer

The truck

The truck is transportation equipment. It can be financed by a term loan to purchase, by a capital lease, or by an operating lease with no purchase guarantee.

The trailer

The trailer is also transportation equipment. The contract says whether it is financed with the truck or separately. This site does not set one rule that would apply to every file.

Who it is for

Businesses that move material

The page is first for businesses in the Transportation sector. A business in another sector on the site, for example construction or agriculture, may also need a truck: the asset remains transportation equipment.

If the truck or the trailer is already paid for and the business wants to review the financing, the file belongs with equipment refinancing, not with a new purchase.

To remember

  • Transportation sector: transportation equipment
  • Truck and trailer, together or separate
  • The same contract logic as other equipment

FAQ

Common questions

Does this page cover both trucks and trailers?

Yes. Both are transportation equipment, the asset already named in the Transportation sector of Versa Capital.

Does the truck and the trailer need one contract?

Not necessarily. They can be combined or kept separate, depending on the project and on what the partners will review. The site does not impose one way of doing it.

Which forms of financing are in the catalogue?

A term loan to purchase, a capital lease, and an operating lease with no purchase guarantee. The detail of leasing is on its own page.

Can an owner in another sector finance a truck?

Yes, if the need is transportation equipment. The industry of the business does not change the nature of the asset.

How does Versa Capital handle the file?

The team understands the intended use, proposes a structure, presents the file to more than 20 financial partners and compares the replies with the business.

Is there a tax treatment specific to trucks?

Versa Capital does not publish one. The treatment depends on the contract and on the business. An accountant or a tax advisor must confirm it before signing.
