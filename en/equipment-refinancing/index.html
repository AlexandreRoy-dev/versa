The product

Two paths already named in the catalogue

The equipment financing page names refinancing, sale-leaseback and renewal strategies. This page describes those paths. It publishes neither an amount nor a residual value.

The starting point is equipment the business already holds, or an equipment contract already under way. A new purchase is treated instead as equipment financing or leasing.

The two paths

Sale-leaseback and renewal

Sale-leaseback

The business sells equipment it owns, then keeps using it under a new contract with a lessor. The conditions of use and of the end of the contract are the ones that are written.

Renewal of the file

A renewal strategy reviews equipment financing that is already in place. The current contract can be replaced by another structure in the catalogue, if a partner agrees to review it. Nothing is settled before a written offer.

Effect on the books

Accounting and tax, with no opinion

The sale of the asset and the new contract can change how the equipment and the payments appear on the books. Versa Capital does not say whether the transaction creates a gain, an expense or a saving. This is not tax advice.

An accountant or a tax advisor must confirm the treatment before signing. If the need is not an asset, but the operating cycle, the catalogue product is instead a working capital loan.

To remember

  • The file starts from equipment already owned
  • Sale-leaseback: sell, then keep using
  • No amount is announced on this page

FAQ

Common questions

What is a sale-leaseback?

The business sells equipment it owns and continues to use it under a new contract. This is the sale-leaseback already named in the catalogue.

Is refinancing a new purchase?

No. Refinancing and renewal start from an asset already owned or from a contract already in place. Buying an asset the business does not yet have is treated as equipment financing.

Does Versa Capital guarantee an amount released by the sale?

No. No amount is published. The amount, if there is one, depends on the asset, the contract and a written offer from a partner.

Can the form of the contract change at renewal?

It is possible if a partner agrees to review another structure in the catalogue, for example a lease or a term loan. It is not automatic.

Who is this for?

A business that already owns equipment in the sectors Versa Capital finances, from construction to food service.

Is an accounting opinion required?

Yes. The sale and the new contract can change the accounting and tax treatment. An accountant or a tax advisor must confirm it. Versa Capital does not give that opinion.
