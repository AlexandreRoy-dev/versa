The service

What we offer

Leasing, financed purchase, operating lease, refinancing and sale-leaseback, structures with a residual value, and renewal strategies.

Depending on the asset: heavy machinery, trucks and trailers or agricultural equipment. Each structure is reviewed against the project, the business profile, available liquidity, how long the asset will be held, and the long-term objectives.

What is included

  • Access to competitive terms through more than 20 financial partners
  • Flexible structures (leasing, loan, lease-to-own)
  • Short timelines and a straightforward process

Our approach

Support in five steps

  1. 1

    Understand

    Grasp the business, its operations, its industry, its financial position and its ambitions.

  2. 2

    Structure

    Build a financing approach suited to the project, the profile and the client objectives.

  3. 3

    Position

    Prepare the file and present it to the appropriate financial partners, with its strengths made clear.

  4. 4

    Compare and explain

    Compare the options and make their effects easy to understand, so the decision is an informed one.

  5. 5

    See it through

    Coordinate the documentation, the negotiation, the signing and the funding, then remain available after the transaction.
